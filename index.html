Purple Lotus Guild

Learn to code by living the adventure. Write Python to guide your hero through a living fantasy world.

What is Purple Lotus Guild?

Purple Lotus Guild is an educational coding RPG. You don't just click buttons — you write real code to move your hero, fight enemies, craft gear, shop in towns, and explore biomes. The game teaches programming concepts naturally through play: variables, functions, conditionals, loops, and more.

Start in Copper Castle, choose your race and class, then venture into the wilds. Your code is your power.

Key Highlights

Code-Driven Gameplay

Control your hero with Python-like commands. hero.move_right(), hero.attack(), hero.equip("sword") — the world responds to your code.

Three Classes

Warrior (strength & stamina), Archer (dexterity & precision), Mage (intelligence & mana). Each has unique skills and playstyles.

Living World

Towns like Copper Castle and Willow Crossing, multiple biomes, crafting stations, shops, and a growing bestiary.

Educational Lessons

Guided lessons that teach coding concepts in context — from your first print() to inventory management and combat tactics.
