The World

Towns, biomes, and the living landscape of Purple Lotus Guild.

Towns of the Copper Mainland

Your journey begins in Copper Castle. Towns unlock as you progress through the campaign.

Copper Castle

Starting hub. Full crafting stations: forge, sawbench, loom, tannery, alchemy table, rune table. Shops for general goods, jewelry, weapons, armor, and alchemy.

Willow Crossing

Similar full services. Forest and grassland biomes nearby.

Saltmere

Coastal influences. Some stations available.

Cinderwell

Swamp and desert regions. Focused crafting options.

Highpass

Higher level band. Continues the progression across the mainland.

Biomes

The world is divided into distinct biomes with unique descriptions, ambient events, and challenges. Examples include:

  • Forest — Ancient trunks, whispering woods, emerald rainforests, frostpine forests, and more.
  • Grassland & Coastal — Open areas and shorelines near early towns.
  • Swamp & Desert — Harsher environments in later regions.

Each biome has entry text and conditions that affect travel and combat. Your code lets you sense and navigate them.

Activities in Town

Visit shops with hero.enter_shop("weapons"), rest, look around, use crafting stations, and prepare for the next expedition into the wilds. The town system is fully code-driven, matching the educational lessons in the game.
