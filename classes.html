Hero Classes

Choose your path.

Warrior

Primary Stat: STR

Resource: Stamina

A fierce warrior who relies on raw strength and stamina. High HP and defense. Skills like Power Strike, Brace, Bash, and Cleave.

High strength and HP, great for front-line combat.

Archer

Primary Stat: DEX

Resource: Stamina

Precise and agile. Excels at ranged combat and mobility. Weapon skills with bows and daggers, plus class abilities focused on accuracy and evasion.

High dexterity for critical hits and positioning.

Mage

Primary Stat: INT

Resource: Mana

Masters of arcane power. Lower physical stats but devastating magical abilities. Uses foci, tomes, and spells drawn from the skill trees.

High intelligence for powerful spells and crafting.

Races

Your race affects starting attributes and profession experience bonuses.

Human

Versatile and adaptable. Excels at learning all professions. +5% all profession XP.

Elf

Graceful masters of magic, archery, and craftsmanship. Bonuses to woodworking and enchanting.

Dwarf

Legendary miners, armorers, and blacksmiths. Strong metalworking bonus.

Orc

Powerful warriors and laborers.

Gnome

Inventors, scholars, and magical researchers.

Beastfolk

Natural hunters and survivalists.

Draken

Descendants of ancient dragons.

How Classes Work

Each class has a skill tree with abilities unlocked by level. Weapon skills are separate and depend on what you equip (daggers, swords, bows, staves, etc.). Resource types (stamina, mana) determine how you fuel abilities.
